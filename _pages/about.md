### 🔬 Research Experience

**LLM Bias Against Autistic Communication**
*Tufts University | Under Submission, CHI'27*
<br>

* Investigated how large language models evaluate autistic communication styles in cross-neurotype social settings.
* Co-designed a dataset of **171 paired dialogues** representing autistic and allistic communication across three scenarios involving cross-neurotype communication breakdowns.
* Evaluated **9 LLMs** across four decision-making tasks, finding systematic penalties against autistic communication styles across evaluation settings.
* Applied ordinal logistic regression, cluster-robust standard errors, Wald tests, and false discovery rate (FDR) corrections to quantify differences in model evaluations.
* Conducted reflexive thematic analysis of seven think-aloud dataset review sessions and semi-structured interviews with autistic participants.

*Keywords: AI Fairness, Accessibility, Neurodiversity, LLM Evaluation, Statistical Modeling*

**Software Reliability & Automated Program Repair**

* Investigated test flakiness by executing tests across **1,000 runs**, analyzing inconsistent pass/fail behavior, execution logs, and reproducibility.
* Executed test suites across **59 repositories** from DS-APACHE and DS-GITHUB to investigate failures caused by obsolete tests.

*Keywords: Software Engineering, Automated Program Repair, Test Flakiness, Software Reliability*

**Distributed Optimization & Multi-Agent Systems**
*Information Technology University | Under Dr. Usama Mehmood*
<br>

* Developed a distributed Model Predictive Control (MPC) framework for coordinating autonomous agents under system dynamics and safety constraints.

* Analyzed convergence, constraint satisfaction, stability, and scalability across varying numbers of agents.
* Evaluated performance under model uncertainty and external disturbances, comparing distributed and centralized MPC.

*Keywords: Model Predictive Control, Distributed Optimization, ADMM, Multi-Agent Systems*

**Mental Health AI & Digital Privacy**
